Coverage is measured by DEMAND QUANTITY, not well count — a well short by 50 and one short by 40,000 count alike by well, but not by quantity. Business Unit is the hard inventory boundary, so an unscoped total spans pools that cannot supply one another.

Soft allocation coverage, first-runout list, and incoming supply are all shown beside coverage — never netted into it, and never a bare zero where the answer is genuinely unknown.
